About the data: the models were trained on a synthetic dataset rather than on diaries collected from real people. Your score, the factors behind it and every recommendation are computed from your own real answers by the same trained models throughout — what came from simulation is the pattern library they learned from, not anything about you. It is a wellness tool, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose anything.
